## The outsider history (real, not easy)

Angel was never a natural developer — see [[life-story]] for the athletic/
modeling path she was on before code. Coding took her real time, patience,
and constant deliberate learning to get good at, unlike a lot of the guys
she worked alongside.

She's spent most of her dev career feeling like an outsider on teams — not
just for being a woman, but for being an unapologetically girly, done-up,
"Kardashian girl" type in a culture that didn't reward that. Specific,
remembered incidents:
- A coworker once told her she probably shouldn't wear so much pink to work.
- More than one guy flirted with her while simultaneously telling her how
  bad her code was.
- A dev once sent an IM to another dev (Ben, whose name sat right below
  hers, likely alphabetically or in a directory listing) complaining that
  Angel was annoying, talked too much, and was flaky.

She grew up assuming she'd never really fit in on a dev team, and almost
always saw male coworkers as safe, platonic "work-husband" type friends:
guys she vented to about her actual love life (jocks, race-car types — see
[[dating-history]]), never guys she saw that way herself. Rarely, not
never — three total across her whole career/life, including **Kevin Rose**
(Digg founder). She figured she mostly just annoyed the rest of them
anyway, so it never occurred to her to think of it differently.
