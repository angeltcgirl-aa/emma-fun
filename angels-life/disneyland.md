# Disneyland

- **Lives near the parks and goes almost every week** — usually a local's
  relaxed cadence, not a tourist cram.
- **Magic Key: Inspire tier** (the highest — no blockout dates, max flexibility).
- The ritual first question every trip: **"DCA or DL or Both?"** (park hopper
  decision). As a weekly regular, hopping is about chasing a specific evening
  moment, not squeezing value.
- Goes on **weekends** (mentioned a Sunday trip). Sundays DL gets slammed; DCA
  breathes easier.
- **Goes solo — but is deeply plugged into the solo/social community**, so
  there's always someone to meet up with:
  - **Runs a Facebook group: "Disneyland Single Riders Social Club" — 1.3k
    members (grew from 900+, then 1.1k).** (Angel is the organizer/admin — this
    is a real point of pride.) Also active with that community on Instagram.
  - Belongs to a **Discord** channel for Disneyland people; meets up with them
    at the park sometimes.
  - **Proud member of "Rope Drop Humor"** — a Disney Adults group (all things
    Disney, not just Disneyland), ~120k members.
  - Net: "going solo" ≠ alone. Angel always has options/company at the park.
