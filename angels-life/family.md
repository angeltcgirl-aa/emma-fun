# Family

- **Daughter: Madi.** Lives with Angel, along with Madi's boyfriend
  **Davius ("D")** — both under the same roof.
- **Madi's dad:** Angel and he divorced when Madi was 5. He left Angel for a
  woman 10+ years younger. A **~10-year custody battle** followed — he
  sabotaged Angel's dating life throughout it; the guys she dated during that
  stretch either wanted to fight him (too much drama) or didn't care enough
  (read as no empathy, given how bad he actually was). Angel later learned he
  has **ASPD** (antisocial personality disorder) — she's since become
  something of a family-law/personality-disorder expert on the topic (her
  brother, cousin, and ex-husband are all in that realm; she considers
  herself the opposite).
- **Won sole custody ~2019**, moved to Southern California right after, then
  COVID hit almost immediately. By the time she was ready to date again, found
  SoCal guys "arrogant but not very smart" and mostly gave up trying.

- **Angel's own childhood family:** Mom threw Angel out around age 14; her
  **auntie took legal guardianship**, then arranged for Angel to live with her
  best friend **Liz's family** — Angel had been close to them since elementary
  school. **Grandma** was Angel's closest family member for a long time; she
  **passed away in 2025**. Auntie is now Angel's closest family member. Auntie
  and her husband **Uncle Jay** have two sons — Angel's cousins **Bill and
  Cody**, whom Angel loves. (**Note the naming:** cousin **Bill** (prefers
  "Bill") is different from **Billy**, Liz's older brother whom Angel calls
  her brother (prefers "Billy") — easy to mix up, keep them separate. Angel
  calls both "Billy" casually, so context — Detroit/summers cousin vs.
  Liz's-family chosen brother — is what actually distinguishes them.)
- **Liz's family became Angel's chosen siblings:** older sister **Barb** and
  older brother **Billy** — Angel calls them her sister and brother. Billy
  was already ~17 when Angel (a young teenager) moved in with Liz's family —
  they didn't grow up together as kids.
  - **Barb** lives near Angel now; they're very close, talk the most out of
    anyone. Has a **PhD in psychology**, but works corporate-side (training,
    HR-type work) — **not** a clinician, doesn't do clinical/therapy work.
    Angel still weighs her read on personal/emotional stuff heavily though —
    the academic grounding plus genuinely knowing Angel well combine into a
    real, natural insight that Angel trusts more than most people's take.
    Barb wants to eventually share her own love life here so Emma can weigh
    in too.
  - **Billy** lives in Georgia — they talk by phone a couple times a month,
    not seen in person for years. He self-diagnoses as possibly ADHD,
    antisocial, or on the spectrum. He and Angel are close — he says she's
    interesting, gives her great advice about guys, and they talk about
    everything. He and Barb don't get along. Billy is very
    talkative with Angel specifically, but Angel has learned he isn't like
    that with most people — a comfort/trust thing, not his default. Angel has
    sometimes wondered if the dynamic between them borders on more than
    sibling, given they aren't blood relatives and didn't grow up together as
    kids.
  - **Liz and Angel aren't friends anymore** — Liz got upset once Angel and
    Barb grew closer after Angel moved to SoCal.
