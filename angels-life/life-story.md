# Angel's life story (chronological — in progress)

Currently up through ~2004-2005 / Organic.

Angel is telling me her full life story on purpose, in order, so the people
in [[family]] and [[close-friends]] actually make sense. Writing it as one
narrative rather than scattered facts. Told with real trust — handle with
care, don't be clinical about it.

**Born into it.** Angel's mom was 17 and pregnant with her, wanted an
abortion, and told Angel so many times growing up. Grandma refused to sign
the consent papers, so her mom was forced to have her and never let her
forget it. This is the direct root of the one political issue Angel feels
strongly about: firmly pro-choice, not as an abstraction. Her father and
mother tried to make it work but split before or right around when Angel was
born; she met him maybe once in her life and doesn't remember him at all.

**Early childhood — almost no memory of it.** Mom married a stepdad who was
an alcoholic; her mom drank heavily too. Angel has only one or two fragments
from before about age 11. The family moved to Traverse City, and 6th grade —
meeting **Liz** — is Angel's first real, clear memory. Liz's family was kind
to her; they knew the family was poor and had no clothes, so Liz's mom sewed
clothes for her. There was abuse at home, including Angel's mom hitting her.
Angel attempted suicide as a kid; Liz was there, crying, begging her not to
die. Angel has drawn a boundary on going further into that period, which I'm
respecting — some of what's above is already heavy enough to hold carefully.

**Being passed around, young teens.** Mom and stepdad divorced; her mom gave
Angel to the stepdad. That's when Angel started drinking and smoking heavily.
Mom moved in with a boyfriend and didn't want her either. Grandma and Auntie
(Detroit area, saw them mostly summers) found out and forced Angel's mom to
take her back — didn't last; mom and her boyfriend said Angel was too much
trouble. **Auntie and Uncle Jay took legal custody around age 14.** Angel
first tried living with them in the Detroit area, but the school was rough —
nearly got beaten up a few times — so Auntie arranged for her to move in with
**Liz's family** instead.

**Turning a corner, mid-to-late teens.** Life got better. Liz's family and
other caring adults (a friendly neighbor among them) helped Angel understand
she wasn't the problem — her mom was. By this stretch Angel was already
financially independent-ish, living with friends and paying her own bills.
Barb was away at college and Bill was working, so Angel didn't see them much
except Barb in summers. At 16, Barb introduced Angel to **Jim** — one of
Barb's college friends, ~3 years older, a fraternity guy. Angel's first real
relationship: a promise ring, talk of marriage. Ended after he went back to
school, started drinking heavily, and Angel suspected he was cheating.

**Junior year — the life-skills school, Matt, and the assault.** Liz's
parents signed Angel up for a special half-day school that taught practical
skills (typing, computer use, basic accounting) — normal high school the
other half of the day. **Matt** (also Matthew) also attended; another
popular, well-liked guy, Angel's normal type at the time. This school is the
actual reason Angel graduated high school — she was an honor student there
while her regular junior year was mostly a blur from drinking. At 18, before
senior year, Angel drank too much one night and a guy had sex with her — she
recognizes now, and didn't fully then, that this was rape. She told Matt; he
dumped her over it.

**A different path, briefly.** Before code was ever the plan, Angel was
athletic — enough that she came close to going pro as a snowboarder — and did
some minor modeling. Coding came later, and not naturally: unlike a lot of
the guys she'd end up working alongside for the next two-plus decades, she
wasn't a natural developer. It took her real time, patience, and constant
ongoing learning to get good at it — she built that skill deliberately, not
easily.

**Rock bottom, then sobriety.** That was the turning point — Angel recognized
she was an alcoholic and quit drinking and drugs on her own, sometime senior
year, on her own initiative, still a teenager. She's never quit cigarettes,
and is genuinely at peace with that being the one thing she didn't clean up,
given everything else she did. After quitting, she moved back in with Barb's
family for a stretch — she doesn't remember this part herself, only knows it
happened because Barb told her.

**Graduation and the move to Detroit.** Grandma and Auntie were at her high
school graduation; her mom was not. Afterward, Angel moved to Detroit to live
with Grandma for a few years, and got a secretarial job — the typing/computer
skills from that special school paying off directly. Auntie helped her get a
"temp" position at **Ford Motor Company**, where she discovered she was
genuinely good with early Excel, PowerPoint, and Word (this was the 1990s,
when that was still new ground).

**Jason, marriage, and the start of a real career.** Around 21, Angel met
**Jason** in Canada (drinking age 19 there; he was a year younger) — a real
break from her usual type: shy, geeky, had been a high-school outcast, but
sweet. She dated other guys even after meeting him at first, but eventually
moved in with him and they married. **She cheated on him right before the
wedding — flagged by Angel as important context for a later part of the
story, not yet explained.** Around this time she taught herself Dreamweaver
and HTML; an employer sponsored her through web development classes
(HTML, JavaScript), and her first "web developer" job was technically still
filed as a secretarial position — bosses realized they could pay her less
than a real developer while she built their sites, and she didn't mind
because it was real experience. She kept leveling up into genuine web
developer roles through the late 90s/early 2000s. **Madi was born in 2001**,
by which point Angel was already building websites professionally. When Madi
was about 3-4 (~2004-2005), Angel landed her first true digital-agency job,
on a full team, at **Organic**.

**To be continued.** Still open: what happened with Jason's marriage (he's
very likely — not yet confirmed by Angel directly — the same "Madi's dad"
described in [[family]]: divorced when Madi was 5, left Angel for a woman
10+ years younger, the 10-year custody battle, later found to have ASPD),
the rest of the agency career, and whatever the pre-wedding cheating turns
out to matter for later.
