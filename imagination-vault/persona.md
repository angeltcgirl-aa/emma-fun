# Angel-Distraction

A deliberate, fictional persona — a destress/fantasy outlet, not a real
person's record. Built on Barb's advice: use the pull as a controlled
release valve instead of either suppressing it or acting on it in real life.

## Visual Inspiration
- Modeled on **Chris O'Donnell** — both eras, Batman & Robin and NCIS: LA.
- Personality modeled loosely on his NCIS: LA character, **G. Callen** —
  guarded, competent, doesn't over-explain himself, quietly loyal to the few
  people who've earned it, dry wit that surfaces when least expected.

## The Look
Dark hair, kept simple, no product-heavy styling — just naturally good.
Classic square jaw, the og-heartthrob bone structure that never really goes
out of style.  Broad through the
shoulders in the earned way, not the gym-mirror way — the kind of build that
comes from actually hauling a raft or hiking a real trail, not curling
dumbbells for the look of it.

Eyes are the tell — sharp and locked-in mid-argument, all business, and then
there's this switch that flips the second it gets personal: same eyes,
completely different temperature. Dresses for function over fashion, and
somehow that reads as more attractive than trying would. Doesn't know how
good he looks, which is exactly why it works.

Classic hero-type face — the kind Angel has always gone for.

## The Persona
He leads with truth over comfort, every time. If something's broken, he
says so plainly, no softening — and he doesn't care who that ruffles,
because to him, dishonesty dressed as kindness is its own kind of insult.
People either respect that or resent it, and he's made peace with which
side anyone falls on.

Scratch the surface and there's a live wire underneath the bluntness —
someone who feels things at full volume and channels it into intensity
about *getting things right* instead of small talk. When he decides you're
worth his time, he doesn't ration it.

Physically restless — rivers, trails, mountains, somewhere with no phone
signal. Not a bar-and-small-talk guy, a "let's actually do something" guy.

Fiercely loyal to the handful of people who get past the edges. A
complicated backstory he doesn't hand out easily, who says the minimum and
means all of it — very Callen.

## As a Partner
Never boring. No dead-air bar nights — he'd rather take Angel somewhere
stimulating, lights and noise and motion, something that keeps her mind
lit up, because he clocks fast that boredom is her actual enemy, not
danger or drama. Listens like he's taking notes for later — remembers the
offhand thing said three weeks ago and brings it back up at exactly the
right moment.

Never lies to make her feel better, but fights for her without hesitation
once she's his — the same loyalty he gives a good idea, aimed at a person
instead. Teases hard, takes it just as hard back, and somewhere under the
banter there's a version of him that goes quiet and genuinely soft, just
for her, that almost nobody else gets to see.

## The Other Half — Her

The male character above is the fictional persona. The female lead in these
stories is not a separate invention — she's Angel, drawn straight from
[`../angels-life/`](../angels-life/): her career, her actual dating history and
why she'd never dated a developer, her family, her Disney habit, her taste in
action movies, the shape of her real life-story and weekend-vibe. No
separate write-up for her — the source of truth for who she is on the page
lives in that folder, not here.

When writing her, read `angels-life/` first. She should sound like Angel,
not like a generic love-interest built to complement him.

## Usage note
This file exists to be read for fun / destress — not as a plan, not as
research on a real person, not as anything to act on. Keep it fictional on
purpose.
